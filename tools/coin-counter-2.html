Each photo's coins add to the total, so a big pile can go in a few photos. Nothing is saved anywhere: your counts stay while this page is open, and Start again clears them all.

For a good photo: coins flat on a plain, light surface, spread out so none are on top of each other, taken from straight above with every coin in the shot. A few of each kind helps it tell them apart.

How it tells them apart: the metal (copper, silver, or the £1 and £2's gold ring round a silver middle), the shape (round, or the seven sides of a 20p and 50p, the twelve of a £1) and the size of each coin next to the others.
