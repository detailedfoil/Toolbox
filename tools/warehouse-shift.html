    How to play

    For hands-on practice, choose Play tutorial on the home screen. Follow the floor markers and the amber outline on the next control.

    Lock up to see the day’s stats and team score out of 1,000. Choose Next for upgrades, then Start Next Day to begin in the office. Quick greetings, short customer waits, prompt picks and speedy putaway all count. The timers use playing time. A missed delivery puts more weight on customer waits.

    Human teammates share 5 XP per greeting, 15 per sale, 10 per confirmed pick line, 10 per finished order and 10 per shelved carton. Once you contribute, team work XP is saved as you play. Spend it at the office Manager PC under Upgrades. Lock-up adds a shift bonus of up to 25%. Work done while a teammate or Sam stands at a till earns 5% extra XP, including its share of the shift bonus. Standing cover counts as contributing. Service Champ XP is rounded across the shift and saved on this device.

    Sam starts with all four jobs enabled. He serves customers first, then picks customer orders and holding-area orders, then puts away delivery. Buy speed upgrades under Upgrades on the Manager PC. Online shifts use the host’s Sam speed. Two saved job lists unlock at 350 lifetime XP.

    The pick list

    • Your scanner can disconnect mid-job. If it shows Server connection issue, go to the office. Face the charging dock beside the manager’s PC and use Swap scanner. Your replacement keeps your task and quantities and is protected for 60 seconds of shift time. Once the final line is confirmed, you can hand over the order without another disconnect.
    • The scanner opens on its Main Menu: Putaway or Today Picking. Back goes back to it.
    • The scanner lists today's picks: A4821 2:3 CK is order A4821, 2 locations, 3 items. CK: a click and collect customer is at the counter. c: nobody's here yet, it goes in the holding area. Nothing after it: bought at the till, the customer's waiting.
    • Up and down choose an order, Enter starts it. Once started it's yours: Back says Finish task.
    • A full day is 15 to 25 picks, plus the delivery. Day one has about a third of the usual delivery and customers. It ends when they're all done, the delivery's away and nobody's waiting: then lock the front door.

    Picking

    • The scanner shows one location at a time: the location, the SKU, the product and how many.
    • Walk to it, look at it and press Scan (or tap it) once for each item: Qty 3 is three scans. Then press Enter for the next location.
    • Then take the tote to the trade counter and press Enter: its customer comes over for it.
    • A c pick goes in the holding area by goods in: scan the label of any empty OC spot.
    • Some click and collects are already bagged in the holding area: the line on the scanner gives the OC location (big green label) and surname. Scan that tote, then take the bag to the counter.
    • Bottom shelves are low: squat to scan them. The high ones (AA shelf 13 and up, BA shelf 7 and up, the top BB level): take a red kick stool from the end of a row, carry it into the aisle and Step up. Stuck? Hint makes the location glow.

    Putaway

    • When a customer brings something from the shop floor, the till beeps and adds it automatically as Picked. On the scanner, choose Shop floor replen, pick the replacements and use Restock shelf at the middle shop-floor unit. Each unit earns 10 XP there. Finish every replen batch before locking the front door.
    • The delivery waits at goods in on the roll cages and pallets. With no picks to do, choose Putaway.
    • Look at a cage or pallet and Scan: you take a carton and the scanner says where it goes. Scan that location, then Enter to put it on the shelf.
    • To stop putaway, press Back, carry the carton back to Goods In and scan its original cage or pallet. Sam finishes the cartons he takes.
    • Putting cartons away advances the clock and brings in customers. By halfway through delivery, roughly half can arrive. The final customers can arrive with one carton left. If the delivery is cancelled, the wall clock moves from 07:00 to 20:00 as you hand orders to customers.

    Sam

    • Sam works from the same list, at his current speed tier. What Sam does first is set on the PC on the manager's desk in the office: tick or untick each job (till service, customer picks, holding-area picks, delivery) and move them up or down. Unticked jobs are yours.
    • If a customer has waited about six seconds and Sam is within reach, he rings the service bell. Ring it yourself to call for help on tills. Everyone in co-op hears it. If till service is enabled, Sam finishes shelving any carton he’s carrying, then helps the waiting customer before scanning another carton.
    • Sam pushes a trolley. While they pick it blocks the aisle, so you can't get past. Sam won't go down an aisle you're in.
    • Sam can take your scanner by mistake while you serve customers at the till. Expect 2–4 mix-ups per shift with an unlabelled scanner and 0–1 with a name label. The message appears after you close the till. Walk up to Sam to get yours back.

    At the till

    Service Champ +5% XP applies to work done while someone covers the staff area behind the counter, even while they move.

    • Face the queue and press Greet for the longest-waiting customer within view and reach. Aim directly at someone else to greet them first. The idle scanner shows the status of customers you look at near the tills. Customers ordering at the till walk to a free till, or wait in line until one opens. Follow them and press Use till (or Scan). You can greet one till customer at a time. Another player or Sam can take over by serving them. The till by the gap is Sam's.
    • Enter their surname, then their postcode in Fusion’s Identify Customer. Press Search, listen for their third detail, then select the matching record.
    • Sent it from the kiosk? They tell you the name they used: open Kiosk Orders, pick theirs, Select, and the basket fills itself.
    • They ask for one thing at a time: a code, or the product’s name with a detail such as its size or brand. Find it on the CrashFix tab (tap a result to read its description) and tap its code to copy it: the customer says straight away if it's the one. Any product that fits counts.
    • Back on the Fusion tab, type the part number and quantity, Enter. Wrong quantity? Type the right one in that line's Qty box. Ask again repeats what they want, twice gives a clue.
    • If a line shows Avail 0, listen to the customer. Want it tomorrow? Press the green Today button to switch it to orange TCND. If they decline, use × to remove that line. Only today's warehouse items go on the customer pick.
    • When they've got everything: Pay & Go, then Card or Cash, whichever they say. The order goes on the pick list (its number stays on the till's screen) and the customer waits by the gap at the end of the counter.
    • For cash, check the change due. Tap the notes and coins in the drawer, then Give change. Exact money completes the sale automatically.
    • On a phone, Fusion uses the phone's keyboard; the keyboard button at the top swaps to an on-screen one.

    Reading a location

    • AAB-03-07-02: area AAB, bay 03, shelf 07, position 02. The area is on the end of the racking.
    • Bay 01 is at the centre aisle end. Shelf 01 is the bottom. Positions count from the left as you face the shelf.
    • Trays hold 2 positions (4 in AAA), listed on the label with the back one on top.
    • Right side, front to back: AAA to AAE (trays), then BAA to BAG. Left side, BBA at the front to BBJ at the back (bulky). Picks go up the right, then down the left from BBJ to BBA.

    Phone

    • Left thumb: walk (push further to jog)
    • Drag the view to look round
    • Tap a label to scan it
    • Tap Squat for low shelves. For high shelves, face a red kick stool and tap Take stool. Carry it to the shelf and tap Step

    Controller

    • Left stick walk (push right out to jog), right stick look
    • RT scan, A Enter, B Back, X squat, LB step on the stool, Y hint
    • D-pad: up and down the pick list
    • Start: enlarge the blue scanner buttons
    • At the till: the d-pad moves the highlight, A presses (a box brings up the keyboard), B deletes, LT/RT switch tabs (LT Fusion, RT CrashFix), LB Enter, Y Ask again, Start leaves

    Computer

    • WASD walk, Shift jog
    • Click to look with the mouse. Esc pauses, including in Fusion and on the Manager PC
    • Click or press F to interact with the outlined target or scan. Enter confirms, C squats, and Space steps onto or off a kick stool
    • Scroll the scanner with the mouse wheel or ↑↓. Right-click or Backspace goes back on the scanner
    • Press Tab or Function to enlarge the blue scanner buttons. Use 1 for Hint and 2 for Quick Pick when shown. Press a number again if you used it to enlarge the buttons.
