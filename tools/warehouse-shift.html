Warehouse Shift

Customers order at the trade counter. Pick each line from its location, one at a time, then bring the tote back and hand it over. The scanner sends you up the right side and back down the left.

A made-up warehouse with the store's 10,999 nailed products. Best played with your phone sideways.

How to play

Picking

  • The scanner shows one pick at a time: the location, the SKU, the product and how many.
  • Walk to the location, look at it and press Scan (or tap it). Type the quantity, press Enter.
  • After the last pick, take the tote to the trade counter and press Enter to hand it over.
  • Bottom shelves are low: squat to scan them. Stuck? Hint makes the location glow.

Reading a location

  • AAB-03-07-02: area AAB, bay 03, shelf 07, position 02. The area is on the end of the racking.
  • Bay 01 is at the centre aisle end. Shelf 01 is the bottom. Positions count from the left as you face the shelf.
  • Trays hold 2 positions (4 in AAA), listed on the label with the back one on top.
  • Right side, front to back: AAA to AAE (trays), then BAA to BAG. Left side, back to front: BBA to BBJ (bulky).

Phone

  • Left thumb: walk (push further to jog)
  • Drag the view to look round
  • Tap a label to scan it
  • Squat button next to the scanner

Computer

  • WASD walk, Shift jog
  • Click to look with the mouse, Esc lets go
  • Click or F scan, C squat, H hint
  • Type the quantity, then Enter
  • Tab opens the scanner's Function menu
