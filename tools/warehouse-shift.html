    Warehouse Shift

    A day is 20 picks on one list, shared with Sam. Choose an order on the scanner, pick each location, then take the tote to its customer at the counter, or put a c pick away in the holding area. Customers come to the trade counter too: some order at the till, where you find what they ask for on CrashFix and add it in Fusion.

    A made-up warehouse with the store's 10,999 nailed products. Best played with your phone sideways.

    Two players

    Split screen, one above the other, sharing the same pick list (and Sam). Each player presses something to join: A on a controller, or any key or a click for the keyboard and mouse. The first to join plays in the top half.

    How to play

    The pick list

    • The scanner lists today's picks: A4821 2:3 CK is order A4821, 2 locations, 3 items. CK: a click and collect customer is at the counter. c: nobody's here yet, it goes in the holding area. Nothing after it: bought at the till, the customer's waiting.
    • Up and down choose an order, Enter (or Space) starts it. Once started it's yours: Back says Finish task.
    • A day is 20 picks. It ends when they're all done and nobody's waiting.

    Picking

    • The scanner shows one location at a time: the location, the SKU, the product and how many.
    • Walk to it, look at it and press Scan (or tap it) once for each item: Qty 3 is three scans. Then press Enter for the next location.
    • Then take the tote to the trade counter and press Enter: its customer comes over for it.
    • A c pick goes in the holding area by goods in: scan the label of any empty OC spot.
    • Some click and collects are already bagged in the holding area: the line on the scanner gives the OC location (big green label) and surname. Scan that tote, then take the bag to the counter.
    • Bottom shelves are low: squat to scan them. Stuck? Hint makes the location glow.

    Sam

    • Sam works from the same list, at the same pace as a person: picks with a customer waiting first, then the next customer at the till, then c picks.
    • Sam pushes a trolley. While they pick it blocks the aisle, so you can't get past. Sam won't go down an aisle you're in.
    • Sam walks round you, and says excuse me if you're in the way.

    At the till

    • Some customers order at the till. Walk behind the counter to a till and press Use till (or Scan): the next one comes over. The till by the gap is Sam's.
    • They give their postcode, then their surname: type them in Fusion's Identify Customer, Search, pick them, Select.
    • Sent it from the kiosk? They tell you the name they used: open Kiosk Orders, pick theirs, Select, and the basket fills itself.
    • They ask for one thing at a time: a code, a description, or a quote from the website. Find it on the CrashFix tab (tap a result to read its description) and tap its code to copy it: the customer says straight away if it's the one. Any product that fits counts.
    • Back on the Fusion tab, type the part number and quantity, Enter. Wrong quantity? Type the right one in that line's Qty box. Ask again repeats what they want, twice gives a clue.
    • When they've got everything: Pay & Go, then Card or Cash, whichever they say. The order goes on the pick list (its number stays on the till's screen) and the customer waits by the gap at the end of the counter.
    • On a phone, Fusion uses the phone's keyboard; the keyboard button at the top swaps to an on-screen one.

    Reading a location

    • AAB-03-07-02: area AAB, bay 03, shelf 07, position 02. The area is on the end of the racking.
    • Bay 01 is at the centre aisle end. Shelf 01 is the bottom. Positions count from the left as you face the shelf.
    • Trays hold 2 positions (4 in AAA), listed on the label with the back one on top.
    • Right side, front to back: AAA to AAE (trays), then BAA to BAG. Left side, BBA at the front to BBJ at the back (bulky). Picks go up the right, then down the left from BBJ to BBA.

    Phone

    • Left thumb: walk (push further to jog)
    • Drag the view to look round
    • Tap a label to scan it
    • Squat button next to the scanner

    Controller

    • Left stick walk (push right out to jog), right stick look
    • RT scan, A Enter, B Back, X squat, Y hint
    • D-pad: up and down the pick list
    • Start: the Function menu
    • At the till: the d-pad moves the highlight, A presses (a box brings up the keyboard), B deletes, LT/RT switch tabs (LT Fusion, RT CrashFix), LB Enter, Y Ask again, Start leaves

    Computer

    • WASD walk, Shift jog
    • Click to look with the mouse, Esc lets go
    • Click or F scan, Space (or Enter) next, C squat, H hint
    • ↑↓ choose on the pick list
    • Tab opens the scanner's Function menu
