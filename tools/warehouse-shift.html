    Warehouse Shift

    A day is 15 to 25 picks on one list, shared with Sam. Choose an order on the scanner, pick each location, then take the tote to its customer at the counter, or put a c pick away in the holding area. Customers come to the trade counter too: some order at the till, where you find what they ask for on CrashFix and add it in Fusion.

    A made-up warehouse with the store's 10,999 nailed products. Play with your phone sideways or upright, and turn it whenever you like.

    Online co-op

    Two players

    Split screen, side by side, sharing the same pick list (and Sam). Each player presses something to join: A on a controller, or any key or a click for the keyboard and mouse. The first to join plays on the left.

    How to play

    Lock up to earn a team score out of 1,000. Quick greetings, short customer waits, prompt picks and speedy putaway all count. The timers use playing time. A missed delivery puts more weight on customer waits.

    Human teammates share 5 XP per greeting, 15 per sale, 10 per confirmed pick line, 10 per finished order and 10 per shelved carton. Contribute to the shift to collect the shared XP plus up to 25% at lock-up. Work done while a teammate or Sam stands at a till earns 5% extra XP, including its share of the shift bonus. Standing cover counts as contributing. Rewards are rounded at lock-up and saved on this device.

    Sam starts with one active job. Swap jobs freely on the manager PC. Buy permanent second, third and fourth job slots for 100, 250 and 500 XP. Online shifts use the host’s training. Two saved job lists unlock at 350 lifetime XP.

    The pick list

    • If your scanner disconnects, go to the office. Face the charging dock beside the manager’s PC and use Swap scanner. Your replacement keeps your current task and quantities.
    • The scanner opens on its Main Menu: Putaway or Today Picking. Back goes back to it.
    • The scanner lists today's picks: A4821 2:3 CK is order A4821, 2 locations, 3 items. CK: a click and collect customer is at the counter. c: nobody's here yet, it goes in the holding area. Nothing after it: bought at the till, the customer's waiting.
    • Up and down choose an order, Enter (or Space) starts it. Once started it's yours: Back says Finish task.
    • A day is 15 to 25 picks, plus the delivery. It ends when they're all done, the delivery's away and nobody's waiting: then lock the front door.

    Picking

    • The scanner shows one location at a time: the location, the SKU, the product and how many.
    • Walk to it, look at it and press Scan (or tap it) once for each item: Qty 3 is three scans. Then press Enter for the next location.
    • Then take the tote to the trade counter and press Enter: its customer comes over for it.
    • A c pick goes in the holding area by goods in: scan the label of any empty OC spot.
    • Some click and collects are already bagged in the holding area: the line on the scanner gives the OC location (big green label) and surname. Scan that tote, then take the bag to the counter.
    • Bottom shelves are low: squat to scan them. The high ones (AA shelf 13 and up, BA shelf 7 and up, the top BB level): take a red kick stool from the end of a row, carry it into the aisle and Step up. Stuck? Hint makes the location glow.

    Putaway

    • The delivery waits at goods in on the roll cages and pallets. With no picks to do, choose Putaway.
    • Look at a cage or pallet and Scan: you take a carton and the scanner says where it goes. Scan that location, then Enter to put it on the shelf.
    • Some shifts start with a notice that applies all day. If the delivery is cancelled, the wall clock moves from 07:00 to 20:00 as you hand orders to customers.

    Sam

    • Sam works from the same list, at the same pace as a person. What Sam does first is set on the PC on the manager's desk in the office: tick or untick each job (customer picks, the queue, holding area picks, the delivery) and move them up or down. Unticked jobs are yours.
    • If a customer waits about six seconds for help, Sam rings the service bell. Ring it yourself to call for help on tills. Everyone in co-op hears it, and Sam prioritises a waiting till customer when assigned to the queue.
    • Sam pushes a trolley. While they pick it blocks the aisle, so you can't get past. Sam won't go down an aisle you're in.
    • Sam has a 40% chance of taking your scanner by mistake when you serve a Fusion customer. A scanner name label halves that to 20%. Finish the sale and walk up to Sam to get your scanner back.

    At the till

    • Face a waiting customer and press Greet to hear what they need. Customers ordering at the till walk to a free till, or wait in line until one opens. Follow them and press Use till (or Scan). You can greet one till customer at a time. Another player or Sam can take over by serving them. The till by the gap is Sam's.
    • Enter their surname, then their postcode in Fusion’s Identify Customer. Press Search, listen for their third detail, then select the matching record.
    • Sent it from the kiosk? They tell you the name they used: open Kiosk Orders, pick theirs, Select, and the basket fills itself.
    • They ask for one thing at a time: a code, or the product’s name with a detail such as its size or brand. Find it on the CrashFix tab (tap a result to read its description) and tap its code to copy it: the customer says straight away if it's the one. Any product that fits counts.
    • Back on the Fusion tab, type the part number and quantity, Enter. Wrong quantity? Type the right one in that line's Qty box. Ask again repeats what they want, twice gives a clue.
    • When they've got everything: Pay & Go, then Card or Cash, whichever they say. The order goes on the pick list (its number stays on the till's screen) and the customer waits by the gap at the end of the counter.
    • For cash, check the change due. Tap the notes and coins in the drawer, then Give change. Exact money completes the sale automatically.
    • On a phone, Fusion uses the phone's keyboard; the keyboard button at the top swaps to an on-screen one.

    Reading a location

    • AAB-03-07-02: area AAB, bay 03, shelf 07, position 02. The area is on the end of the racking.
    • Bay 01 is at the centre aisle end. Shelf 01 is the bottom. Positions count from the left as you face the shelf.
    • Trays hold 2 positions (4 in AAA), listed on the label with the back one on top.
    • Right side, front to back: AAA to AAE (trays), then BAA to BAG. Left side, BBA at the front to BBJ at the back (bulky). Picks go up the right, then down the left from BBJ to BBA.

    Phone

    • Left thumb: walk (push further to jog)
    • Drag the view to look round
    • Tap a label to scan it
    • Tap Squat for low shelves. For high shelves, face a red kick stool and tap Take stool. Carry it to the shelf and tap Step

    Controller

    • Left stick walk (push right out to jog), right stick look
    • RT scan, A Enter, B Back, X squat, LB step on the stool, Y hint
    • D-pad: up and down the pick list
    • Start: the Function menu
    • At the till: the d-pad moves the highlight, A presses (a box brings up the keyboard), B deletes, LT/RT switch tabs (LT Fusion, RT CrashFix), LB Enter, Y Ask again, Start leaves

    Computer

    • WASD walk, Shift jog
    • Click to look with the mouse, Esc lets go
    • Click or F scan, Space (or Enter) next, C squat, V step on the stool, H hint
    • ↑↓ choose on the pick list
    • Tab opens the scanner's Function menu
